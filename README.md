# Among Blocks 👾🧱

A fun, kid-friendly fan **mashup of Among Us and Roblox** — a single static page with original
artwork (no copyrighted game assets).

## Features
- 🎨 **Make Your Crewmate** — pick a colour, roll a silly name
- 🔎 **Find the Impostor** — a "spot the odd one" game that gets harder each level
- 📋 **Do Your Tasks** — click tasks to fill the crew's task bar
- 🧱 **The Blocky Obby — a full Mario-style platformer!**
  - Long side-scrolling level with pits, pipes, brick & "?" blocks
  - Collect 🪙 coins, bonk blocks, stomp 👾 enemies
  - Moving platform over lava, checkpoint flags, staircase climbs
  - High jumps (tap Space for a short hop, hold for a big leap)
  - 🏁 Flagpole finish with score, coins and time
- 🚨 **Emergency Meeting**, confetti, bobbing animations, lots of colour

## Play locally
Open `index.html`, or `python3 -m http.server 8000`.
Just for fun — not an official game. All art is drawn from simple shapes.
