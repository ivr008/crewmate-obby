# Among Blocks 👾🧱

A fun, kid-friendly fan **mashup of Among Us and Roblox** — a single static page with original
artwork and original chiptune music (both generated in-browser; no copyrighted assets).

## Features
- 🎨 **Make Your Crewmate** — pick a colour, roll a silly name
- 🔎 **Find the Impostor** — a "spot the odd one" game that gets harder each level
- 📋 **Do Your Tasks** — click tasks to fill the crew's task bar
- 🍄 **The Blocky Obby — a 4-level Mario-style platformer**
  - 🎵 **Original chiptune music** (Web Audio API) + sound effects (jump, coin, stomp, clear)
  - 4 progressively harder levels: **Green Hills → Crystal Caves → Frozen Peaks → Lava Castle**
  - Collect 🪙 coins, bonk **?** blocks, stomp 👾 enemies, ride moving platforms, dodge lava
  - ❤️ 3 lives, checkpoint flags, high jumps (tap = short hop, hold = big leap)
  - 🏁 Flagpole finish per level with time, coins and score
- 🚨 **Emergency Meeting**, confetti, bobbing animations, lots of colour

## Play locally
Open `index.html`, or `python3 -m http.server 8000`.
Just for fun — not an official game. All art and music are made from scratch.
